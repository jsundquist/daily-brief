Type: grilling

## Question

What does 'really pay attention' mean for you? Settle the topics you want to go deeper on, what you don't care about, and the criteria separating must-see, worth-a-skim, and skip. Decide the format of the interest profile you will maintain.

## Resolution

Ruled out of scope for v1: the user wants v1 to aggregate everything with no LLM judging or interest profile, then add filtering later based on real use. Answers given before the pivot, kept for when tiering returns:

- Core topics: AI/agentic, platform engineering/DevOps, Kubernetes/cloud-native, IaC, testing, Go and other languages (JS/Node, Python), engineering leadership.
- Learning-gap boost: yes, via a short "currently learning" list (Kubernetes, Go).
- Tiers: must-see = major release/shift in a core topic, closes a learning gap, or a hot trend; worth-a-skim = relevant but incremental; skip = the rest.
- Skip list caveats: generic product launches are sometimes wanted, and repeats are fine when they add new context. Do not treat either as an automatic skip.
- Judge looks at item content only (no source weighting); profile format deferred.
